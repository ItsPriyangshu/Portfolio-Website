Here are some types of questions users might ask your AI chatbot, categorized by topic for better organization:

1. Personal & Background Questions
"Tell me about yourself."
"What's your educational background?"
"Where are you based?"
"How many years of experience do you have?"
"What are your hobbies?"

2. Skills & Expertise
"What programming languages do you know?"
"Are you familiar with machine learning?"
"Do you have Android development experience?"
"What frameworks/tools do you use?"
"Can you work with databases?"

3. Projects & Portfolio
"What projects have you worked on?"
"Show me your Python projects."
"Do you have any open-source contributions?"
"Where can I see your GitHub?"
"What was your most challenging project?"

4. Services & Pricing
"What services do you offer?"
"How much does an Android app cost?"
"Do you provide AI solutions?"
"What’s your pricing structure?"
"Do you offer hourly consulting?"

5. Availability & Hiring
"Are you available for freelance work?"
"How can I hire you?"
"What’s your availability timeline?"
"Do you work with international clients?"
"Can you start immediately?"

6. Technical & Career Advice
"How do I start with Python?"
"What’s the future of AI?"
"Should I learn Kotlin or Java for Android?"
"How do you stay updated with tech trends?"
"Tips for building a strong portfolio?"

7. Fun/Casual Questions
"What’s your favorite programming joke?"
"Coffee or tea while coding?"
"Who’s your tech inspiration?"
"What’s your favorite project you’ve built?"
"What do you do when you’re not coding?"
"I love you"

8. Contact & Socials
"What’s your email?"
"Are you on LinkedIn?"
"Can I call you?"
"Where can I follow you?"
"Do you have a resume I can download?"

9. Testimonials & Reviews
"Do you have client testimonials?"
"What do past clients say about you?"
"Can you share references?"
